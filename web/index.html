Football Physics

One button per player. Hold to wind up the kick, release to kick and hop. A quick tap is a small hop and a light touch; a full charge is a big jump and a hard shot. Players hop the way they lean, so wait for a lean back to hop backwards. First to 5 wins.

Play online

One of you hosts and gets a code. The other types the code in and joins. The host plays Blue, the guest plays Red. Online, the key pointing at your own goal controls your keeper: Blue A keeper, D striker; Red D keeper, A striker. The arrow keys work the same way.
